MAIN QUALITIES OF A GOOD BIKE

A good bike should have several important qualities such as good mileage, strong engine performance, safety, comfort, and reliability. It should provide a smooth and comfortable riding experience with good brakes, strong tyres, and proper suspension. A bike with good build quality is durable and requires less maintenance. Attractive design, modern features, and good resale value are also important. Overall, a good bike should be safe, comfortable, fuel-efficient, reliable, and easy to maintain.

QUALITIES OF BIKE

1. Good Mileage - Better fuel efficiency - Saves money
2. Engine Performance - Smooth engine - Good power - Reliable performance
3. Safety - Good brakes - ABS - Strong tyres - Stable handling
4. Comfort - Comfortable seat - Good riding position - Good suspension
5. Build Quality - Strong frame - Durable materials
6. Low Maintenance - Easy servicing - Affordable maintenance
7. Good Design - Attractive appearance - Modern styling
8. Reliability - Fewer breakdowns - Consistent performance
9. Good Resale Value - Maintains value - Easy to sell
10. Modern Features - Digital display - LED lights - Bluetooth connectivity
